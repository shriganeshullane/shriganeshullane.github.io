Hindustani classical music

I trained in Hindustani classical vocal music for over 12 years under my guru, Pt. Vinayaka Hegde Hirehadda, and completed the Senior examination in Hindustani music. I was also awarded a Government of Karnataka scholarship for Hindustani music.

I've performed in concerts across Uttara Kannada and Bangalore, and travelled widely with my guru for his programmes.
